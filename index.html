About Duto

Duto is a modern student productivity web application designed to help students organize every aspect of their academic life in one place. From task management and study planning to notes, timers, and AI assistance — everything lives in a single, intuitive platform.

It was created because students deserve better than fragmented tools and overwhelming interfaces. We believe productivity software should feel calm, focused, and purpose-built for learning.

Mission

To empower every student with a clear, unified workspace that reduces stress and amplifies academic performance.

Vision

A world where every student has the tools to organize their studies, stay focused, and reach their full potential.
